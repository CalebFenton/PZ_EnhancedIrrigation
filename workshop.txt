description=Get running water back after the taps run dry, with what a Kentucky farm had in 1993: a rain barrel up on a log stand, pipe laid down the garden rows, sinks plumbed to a tank, a sand filter or a wood-fired boiler for drinking water, and a pump on the well. You start with one raised barrel and a line you hold open, bed by bed. Every length of pipe is one you scavenged or fired, every pump seal wears through, and nothing in it knows whether it rained.
description=
description=[b]Build 42.21 or newer.[/b] Single player and multiplayer. Fine to add to a save in progress. Requires [b]cf_commonlib[/b].
description=
description=[b][url=https://github.com/CalebFenton/PZ_EnhancedIrrigation/blob/master/docs/player-guide.md]The player's guide[/url][/b] explains every system: how high a tank has to stand, sizing a line, keeping a filter alive, what a pump lifts, and where to look.

description=[h2]Questions[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Can I add it to my save?[/b] Yes. Barrels already in your world count as tanks at once; the pamphlet and the parts turn up in containers nobody has opened yet.
description=[*][b]Can I remove it?[/b] Not cleanly: what it built into your world goes with it.
description=[*][b]Why is my rooftop barrel's water tainted now?[/b] Vanilla's plumbing purified anything that came through a pipe. Here water keeps its taint until you clean it. Turn off [b]Plumbing keeps taint[/b] on the [b]Enhanced Irrigation[/b] sandbox page and vanilla's [b]Plumb[/b] purifies again; a sink the mod already changed over needs unplumbing and plumbing again.
description=[*][b]Can I make it easier, or harder?[/b] Yes, on the [b]Enhanced Irrigation[/b] sandbox page: how often the pamphlet and the parts turn up, and whether plumbing keeps taint.
description=[*][b]Does it change vanilla farming?[/b] No. Rain and watering cans work as before; only water you run through a line or a sprinkler can drown a bed.
description=[*][b]Do I need Renewable Energy?[/b] No. With it, a battery bank powers your Pump Houses and timers, and bills the pumps like any appliance.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Everything you can find or build[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_EnhancedIrrigation/master/art/promo/images/items.png[/img]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Tanks.[/b] A [b]Tank Stand[/b] lifts a rain barrel a storey, and [b]Plumb to Tank[/b] joins barrels into a cistern with buried pipe, up to 10 tiles.
description=[*][b]Garden lines[/b] of plastic, iron, lead or clay pipe, holed under the plants, with a tap at the tank. Overwater and the roots rot; leave them full in a hard frost and they split.
description=[*][b]Plumbed fixtures.[/b] Sinks, baths, showers, toilets and washing machines run from the tank you pick, each its own.
description=[*][b]Clean water[/b] from a [b]Barrel Filter[/b] or a [b]Brick Filter[/b] that ripens for three days and dies if it dries out, a [b]Filter Crock[/b] you shape and fire, or a [b]Boiler[/b] you keep fed like any fire.
description=[*][b]Pumps.[/b] A [b]Wooden Hand Pump[/b] or an [b]Iron Hand Pump[/b] with a leather, rubber or tarp seal that wears through, and a [b]Pump House[/b] for an [b]Appliance Pump[/b] salvaged from a washer or a found [b]Utility Pump[/b].
description=[*][b]Running it for you.[/b] A [b]Lawn Sprinkler[/b] on a pumped line waters a 5 by 3 patch. A [b]Wind-up Valve[/b] shuts the tap for you, and a [b]Timer Valve[/b] or [b]Sprinkler Timer[/b] opens it at the same hour every day, power permitting. You salvage the [b]Water Valve[/b] for it from a washer.
description=[*][b][i]Cisterns for Kentucky[/i][/b], the extension pamphlet that teaches most of it.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Getting started[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_EnhancedIrrigation/master/art/promo/images/interface.png[/img]
description=
description=[olist]
description=[*]Build a [b]Tank Stand[/b] (it's with the rain collectors in the build menu), or put a barrel on a deck or a floor a storey up.
description=[*]Carry an empty rain barrel to the stand and [b]Set Barrel on Stand[/b]. Open its lid for the rain.
description=[*]Carrying pipe and its joints, right-click the ground beside it and [b]Lay Line[/b] along the bed, on the plants' own squares.
description=[*][b]Drill Holes[/b] under the plants, then [b]Fit Tap[/b] beside the barrel with a Pipe Wrench.
description=[*]Stand at the tap: [b]Water from Tank[/b]. The soil darkens under every hole.
description=[/olist]
description=
description=A tap runs only from a tank at least a storey above it, and a higher one pushes harder. A barrel on the ground waters nothing.
description=
description=[h2]Clean water[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_EnhancedIrrigation/master/art/promo/images/filters.png[/img]
description=
description=Rain comes down tainted, and so does the river. A sand filter is slow, about 100 L a day from a barrel's worth of sand, and alive: give it three days to ripen before you trust it, and water it every day or two, or the film that does the cleaning dies and it starts over. A boiler is quick, about 30 L an hour, but it burns fuel all the while, and ten hours of fire with the drum dry burn it through. Keep the clean tank's lid shut; one careless bucket spoils it.
description=
description=[h2]Pumps[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_EnhancedIrrigation/master/art/promo/images/pumps.png[/img]
description=
description=A hand pump on a well gives clean water forever, at about 60% of your endurance an hour, and the wooden one squeals loud enough to bring company. A Pump House does the work on the grid while it lasts, if it stands in or beside a house, then on a generator's fuel. Run one with nothing to draw and it burns out, and every repair brings it back a little less.

description=[h2]Crafting[/h2]
description=Learn the recipes from [i]Cisterns for Kentucky[/i], by levelling Carpentry, Welding, Masonry, Pottery or Electricity, or by researching a part you found. Builds and crafts need light to see by, and the valves and the Utility Pump a table. Laying pipe, drilling, fitting taps and watering take no learning at all. Every recipe is on [url=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_EnhancedIrrigation/master/art/promo/images/recipes.png]one sheet[/url].
description=
description=[h2]Where to look[/h2]
description=Think about who kept water moving in 1993: farmers and gardeners, plumbers and the hardware store. Pipe is in warehouses and on building sites, the pamphlet on a garden centre's shelves, and the good pumps and timers in town, where the dead are. Every washing machine and dishwasher hides a pump and a valve, if you don't mind the laundry.
