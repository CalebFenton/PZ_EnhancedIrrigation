    craftRecipes are, since magazines and research look recipes up by bare name. A DisplayName is
    looked up, without its spaces, in Recipes.json.

    Each build is taught by Cisterns for Kentucky, a skill or research; vanilla applies an entity
    recipe's AutoLearn nowhere, so shared/EnhancedIrrigation/Recipes.lua does.

    /*
        The sand filters (shared/EnhancedIrrigation/Treatment.lua): raw water poured or piped onto
        the sand seeps through into the basin below, this FluidContainer, which vanilla's menus
        drink, fill and empty from. InputLocked keeps vanilla from pouring into it, so water
        reaches it only through the sand; no rain falls in. The OnCreate gives the built filter its
        record at once.

        Sand and gravel are uses of vanilla's bags, four to a bag, as vanilla's own sand floor and
        clay cement take them; each empty sack comes back. The outlet is any of vanilla's three
        pipes and a joint: the build menu can't ask for the joint only with plastic pipe, and a
        threaded pipe still wants sealing into a barrel's stave.
    */
    entity EnhancedIrrigation_BarrelFilter
    {
        component UiConfig
        {
            xuiSkin = default,
            entityStyle = ES_EnhancedIrrigation_BarrelFilter,
            uiEnabled = false,

    /*
        The Filter Crock (Items.txt, Recipes.txt) set in a bucket: raw water poured into the crock
        seeps through its fired walls into the bucket, this FluidContainer, a common bucket's size
        whatever bucket it stands in, locked and rainless as a filter's basin is. Any empty bucket,
        counted as an item: the game uses up an input that holds a fluid container only then. The

    /*
        The Boilers: a Metal Drum over a firebox. The OnCreate swaps the built object for a vanilla
        fireplace with this entity's components, as vanilla's Barrel Oven becomes one, so the game's
        own Add Fuel, Light and Put Out run it, burn its fuel down and sync it; raw water poured or
        piped into the drum comes out boiled into this FluidContainer while the fire keeps it hot,
        locked and rainless as a filter's basin is, and the drum's size. The OnIsValid keeps it
        outdoors and clear of walls. A pipe in for the raw water and a tap out for the boiled.

        The stone firebox takes bricks or stone blocks, cement to set them and the wrench for the
        pipe fittings; the welded one takes sheet metal, screws and welding as vanilla's Barrel Oven
        does, its torch and rods by uses.

    /*
        The hand pumps (shared/EnhancedIrrigation/Pumps.lua): built beside a well, a tank or a
        trough, or near a river for the hose, or anywhere until one is there. Worked by hand, a
        pump lifts its source's water into the tank its outlet is plumbed to. The OnCreate gives
        the pump its record at once and fits the seal the build took. No container: the water goes
        straight through.

        The seal is the cup on the plunger: any of the three that hold a cup's shape, destroyed as
        it goes in, so leather strips leave no dirty strips behind. The wooden one is a carpenter's
